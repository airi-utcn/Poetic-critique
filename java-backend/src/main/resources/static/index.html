The Critic's Rubric

The backend engine automatically scores your verse across three technical axes to ensure it meets Romantic standards.

Flesch-Kincaid Grade

Threshold: ≤ 8.0. This metric measures syntactic entanglement and vocabulary complexity. If the grade exceeds 8.0, the language is too academic or convoluted. The critic will demand simpler, more direct metaphors.

VADER Sentiment

Range: [-1.0 to +1.0]. Evaluates the emotional polarity of the stanza. Highly negative scores indicate grief and melancholy (favorable for Eminescu style), while highly positive scores indicate joy. Stagnation triggers the critic to demand a shift.

Symbol Saturation

Target: > 10%. Measures the density of specific predefined Romantic symbols (e.g., moon, willow, grief, tear) against the total word count. If saturation drops too low, the verse is deemed too sterile.
