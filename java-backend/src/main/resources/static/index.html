The Critic's Rubric

The backend engine automatically scores your verse across three technical axes based on empirical corpus analysis. The strict thresholds update dynamically depending on the active poetic tradition.

Flesch-Kincaid Grade

Eminescu: 4.82 – 10.48 | Yuan Hongdao: 5.13 – 7.94
Measures syntactic entanglement and vocabulary complexity. The Gong'an school requires tighter, more immediate phrasing, while Romanticism tolerates deeper academic structures. The critic will demand simpler, more direct metaphors if the upper bound is breached.

VADER Sentiment

Eminescu: -0.58 to 0.91 | Yuan Hongdao: -0.55 to 0.88
Evaluates the emotional polarity of the stanza. While both poets explore melancholy, stagnation triggers the critic to demand an affective shift to prevent stylistic fatigue.

Symbol Saturation

Eminescu: > 3.5% | Yuan Hongdao: > 5.7%
Measures the density of specific predefined symbols against total word count using dynamic, tradition-specific lexicons. If saturation drops too low, the verse is deemed too sterile and lacking in either romantic grounding or spontaneous xingling observation.
