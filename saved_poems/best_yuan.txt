In such case when the moon rises above Mount Lu Mountain;
I can only look down upon it through glass windows.
I'm living here like an old man who has come back from his journey,
with many years gone past that I have left behind!
---
He was so beautiful when I first saw him;
How could it be now to part from this face?
All our days together had been as fleeting smoke:
In dreams they linger but vanish again.
As I walked along on this path,
When would my journey end? But now at last
here comes someone from afar; he stops here,
suddenly returning like an old friend.
---
In the middle of my speech about life,
I met him; suddenly I became like an autumn day.
My heart is empty but not really so,
like ice on a winter night that hasn't frozen yet.
---
When I have come to think about what happens here every year,
the sky is all white; there's not even an inch of green anywhere.
My bamboo stalks never grow tall; instead their leaves fall off
and turn brown at dusk on days without rain.
---
